FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I register my GST using your address?+
Yes. Our Virtual Address and Business Starter plans support GST registration, company registration, and business registrations.
What is the total amount I need to pay?+
Each plan shows the complete total including deposit, advance, and GST 18%. Virtual Address: ₹1,499–1,799/month (1 month deposit + 1 month advance + GST) | Business Starter: ₹4,999 one-time + deposit + GST | Day Pass: ₹944 total | Dedicated Desk: ₹11,999–19,999/month depending on tier, plus deposit and GST. Taking multiple seats unlocks a discount — 25% off your 2nd seat, 50% off your 3rd. All amounts are clearly shown before payment.
Is the virtual address valid across India?+
Yes. Businesses from anywhere in India can subscribe and use our Mahim, Mumbai address for all government registrations.
What payment methods do you accept?+
UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, and Wallets — all processed securely via Razorpay.
What are your business hours?+
Open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
What is the cancellation and refund policy?+
Cancellations must be requested at least 36 hours before your booking date. Approved refunds are processed within 7 working days.
Will I get a GST invoice after payment?+
Yes. After payment, you will receive a complete GST tax invoice with INDO GROW's GSTIN 27AZMPM8103H1Z6, which you can download and will also be emailed to you.
What's included in the Business Starter package?+
A one-time ₹4,999 setup covering GST Registration, Udyam (MSME) Registration, Shop & Establishment (Gumasta), TAN, Professional Tax Registration, and Brand Registration — everything a new business typically needs to get compliant, using our address as your registered office.
Can I book a meeting room?+
Yes. Meeting rooms (TV + whiteboard, seats up to 6) are ₹500/hour. Semi-Enclosed Cubicle and Glass Cabin members get 2 and 4 hours included per month.
